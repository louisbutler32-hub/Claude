Voice Studio

Paste a script, pick a voice, and render a voice-over MP3. Each line becomes one take, with a pause between takes.

Cloned Cloning is slow on this machine: about 6 seconds of work per second of audio, so a 1-minute script takes around 6 minutes. More expressiveness gives a more dramatic read. Lower pacing slows the delivery down. With 2 or 3 takes, each line is generated that many times and the studio keeps the take that sounds most like the sample and reads the words correctly. That's better quality but takes 2–3× as long.

Download MP3Line timings are saved next to it as .json.

Clone a voice

Upload 10–20 seconds of one person talking clearly, with no music, other voices or echo. Only the first 20 seconds are used.
