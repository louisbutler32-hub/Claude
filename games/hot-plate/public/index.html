    how to play
    1. Everyone takes a seat. A category comes up, like Name any NBA player.
    2. When the hot plate slides in front of you, type something in the category and serve it to pass it on.
    3. No repeats. Anything already served is off the menu, and wrong guesses show up for the whole table.
    4. Watch the pot: the flames grow and it bubbles up, rattles its lid and spills over as your time runs out, then boils over. Get burned and you lose a life. Common answers speed it up; rare ones cool it down.
    5. Rarer answers score more, and enough points win a life back.
    6. Serve three in a row without a wrong guess for a 🌶️ spicy streak: ×1.5 points, then ×2 from five.
    7. Sign in to keep your points, your daily streak 🔥 and your win streak 👑.
    8. Every plate hides one 💋 chef's kiss: a rare-but-gettable answer worth 250. It's never revealed, so keep guessing.
    9. Last one at the table wins.
