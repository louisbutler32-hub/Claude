    How to play
    1. Everyone sits at the table. A category comes up, like Name any NBA player.
    2. When the plate lands in front of you, type something in the category and hit Serve to pass it on.
    3. No repeats. Anything already served is off the menu, and wrong guesses show up for the whole table.
    4. Watch the clock round the plate. Run out and you get burned: one heart gone. The clock gets shorter every pass.
    5. Rarer answers score more. Enough points buy a heart back.
    6. Last one with hearts wins.
