## Description

```
Can you guess the number from its shadow? 1️⃣2️⃣3️⃣

A mystery shadow rises out of the bushes… which number could it be? Guess along
with us, then count out loud together — one carrot, two tomatoes, three ducks —
all the way up to twelve. Every number you name fills one more space on the
board!

🔢 WHAT YOUR LITTLE ONE WILL LEARN
• The numerals 1 to 12, and what each one looks like
• Counting out loud, one object at a time, with the running total on screen
• Number recognition, by matching each shadow to the number it hides
• That the last number you say is how many there are

👶 Made for toddlers, preschoolers and kindergarten. Nice and slow, with a long
pause after every shadow so there's time to shout out the answer.
```
