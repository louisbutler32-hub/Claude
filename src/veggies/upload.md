### The made-for-kids setting is not optional

Content aimed at under-13s has to be flagged as made for kids under COPPA,
and mis-flagging carries real penalties. Setting it has consequences worth
knowing before you upload:

- comments are disabled, so engagement signals come from watch time alone
- no personalised ads, which cuts RPM substantially against general content
- no end screens, cards, notifications or "save to playlist"

None of that is avoidable for this genre — it is the cost of the audience.

### On the tags

Tags carry much less weight than they used to; the title, the thumbnail and
the first two lines of the description do most of the work. The list above
is worth filling in, but don't spend time tuning it — spend it on the
thumbnail A/B instead.
