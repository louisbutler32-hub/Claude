## Description

Have you ever wondered why there is no bridge between Russia and America? At its narrowest the Bering Strait is only 82 km wide, and in the middle sit two islands just 3.8 km apart: Big Diomede (Russia) and Little Diomede (USA). The International Date Line runs between them, so the Russian island is 21 hours ahead — locals call them Tomorrow Island and Yesterday Island. The water is shallow, only about 55 m deep (the Channel Tunnel runs at 75 m below sea level). The problem is everything else: winter ice floes almost 2 m thick, temperatures approaching −50 °C, and no roads to connect to — the nearest highway is about 1,200 km away on the Alaskan side and 2,000 km on the Russian side. Plans have existed since 1890; Russia backed a $65 billion plan in 2011. Nothing was built. In 1987, swimmer Lynne Cox crossed from Little Diomede to Big Diomede in 2 hours 6 minutes.
