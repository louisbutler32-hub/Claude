## Upload settings

Category **Gaming** (game: Minecraft), language English, uploaded as a
Short (24s, 1080×1920). Audience **not made for kids**.

Thumbnail: `thumbnail-nether.jpg` (`npm run nether:thumb`).

Sound: `npm run nether:mix` builds the track from `public/audio/src/`
(Sneaky Snitch under the build and arrival, Run Amok sped up under the
chase, a synthesised whoosh at the portal crossing and a boom on the
fireball hit), then `npm run nether:audio`.

This one is longer and structured differently from the channel's other
Shorts on purpose: five scenes across a real setting change (overworld →
Nether → overworld) instead of two labelled halves. It carries the same
POV band as the creeper Short up top, plus a small corner caption naming
each of the five scenes underneath it — the band says what the video is,
the corner tag says where you are in it.
