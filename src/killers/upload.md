## Description

Not why they did it — nobody has ever settled that. What actually stopped each one.

Ten cases, in order of the year each one ended, and in almost every case the thing that ended it was small, unrelated, and not the result of anyone working out who he was. A traffic stop. A photograph receipt in the wrong pocket. False number plates checked by a force that was not even on the case. A computer that timestamped every edit its user made. A square of gauze filed away for fourteen years until the science caught up with it. A floppy disk. A third cousin who had mailed off a saliva kit out of curiosity about her ancestors.

Only one of the ten was stopped by an operation built to stop him.

No crime scenes, no re-enactments, no photographs of anyone. This is about the investigations.
