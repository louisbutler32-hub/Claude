## Description

You are asleep. It is half past eleven at night. Under your bed there is
carpet, under the carpet concrete, under the concrete sand — and under the
sand, limestone that has been quietly going away for forty thousand years.

This video walks outward from your feet. The floor. Then the lake in front
of you. Then the hill behind the town. Then a body of water that starts
swinging because of an earthquake two thousand kilometres away. Then the
rock under the whole continent. Then the star. Then something from outside
the solar system entirely.

Along the way: the hole in Florida that took a man out of his own bedroom
and was never emptied. The lake in Cameroon that breathed out in one night.
The man who survived a cloud that destroyed a city in under a minute,
because he happened to be locked in a cell. The aurora that let telegraph
operators disconnect their batteries and keep sending. And the supervolcano
everybody is frightened of, which turns out to be the one you could walk
away from — and the story about it that is not true.

No footage, no stock, no music. Every drawing is made in code. Every
photograph is public domain, credited below.
