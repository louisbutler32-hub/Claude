## Description

```
Can you guess the sea creature from its shadow? 🐙🐬🦀

A mystery shadow rises out of the bushes… what could it be? Guess along with
us, find out where each sea creature lives, and hear the sound it makes! Every
sea creature you name fills one more space on the board — can you collect all 12?

🌊 WHAT YOUR LITTLE ONE WILL LEARN
• 12 sea creature names — octopus, shark, crab, starfish, clownfish, seahorse,
  whale, turtle, dolphin, jellyfish, lobster and squid
• Where sea creatures live — the seabed, the coral, the open sea
• Shape recognition, by matching each shadow to the creature it hides
• Counting from 1 to 12 as the board fills up

👶 Made for toddlers, preschoolers and kindergarten. Nice and slow, with a long
pause after every shadow so there's time to shout out the answer.
```
