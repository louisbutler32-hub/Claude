## Description

```
Can you guess the animal from its shadow? 🐄🦆🦁

A mystery shadow rises out of the bushes… what could it be? Guess along with
us, find out where each animal lives, and listen to the sound it makes! Every
animal you name fills one more space on the board — can you collect all 12?

🐾 WHAT YOUR LITTLE ONE WILL LEARN
• 12 animal names — cow, lion, duck, frog, pig, penguin, owl, elephant,
  sheep, fish, cat and dog
• The sound each one makes — moo, roar, quack, ribbit, oink, hoo hoo and more
• Where animals live — the farm, the pond, a hole in a tree, the icy sea
• Shape recognition, by matching each shadow to the animal it hides
• Counting from 1 to 12 as the board fills up

👶 Made for toddlers, preschoolers and kindergarten. Nice and slow, with a long
pause after every shadow so there's time to shout out the answer.
```
