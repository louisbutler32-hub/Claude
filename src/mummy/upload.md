# 7 Ways to Become a Mummy

## Title

7 Ways to Become a Mummy

Alternates:

- 7 Ways to Never Rot
- Seven Ways to Still Be Here in 4,000 Years

## Description

Seven ways a human body has actually been kept, ranked by how long each one has been proven to work. Ice, peat bog, desert sand, rock salt, Egyptian natron, the Japanese monks who did it to themselves over nine years, and the polymer method invented in 1977.

Every drawing, every sound and the narration are made from scratch for this video.

6:45 7. Plastic — plastination
7:37 The ranking

Sources: Otzi (South Tyrol Museum of Archaeology), Tollund Man (Silkeborg Museum), the Chinchorro mummies (UNESCO / Universidad de Tarapaca), the Chehrabad saltmen (Zanjan), Egyptian embalming (Herodotus, Book II, and modern CT studies), sokushinbutsu (Dainichibo and Churenji temples), plastination (Gunther von Hagens, 1977).
