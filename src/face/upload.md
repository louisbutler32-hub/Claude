## Description

There are mites on your face. Not metaphorically — arachnids, related to spiders and ticks, a fifth of a millimetre long, living head-down in your follicles and eating the oil you make. Nobody knew they were there until 1842.

This is everything that lives on a human face: the two species of Demodex mite, the hundred billion bacteria that hold your skin at pH 5, the fungus that causes dandruff and is on virtually every adult on Earth, and the viruses that hunt the bacteria. Plus why washing does not get rid of any of it, and why the regulators pulled antibacterial soap in 2016.

None of which you can feel, and all of which were there before you were.

Every drawing and the narration are made from scratch for this video.
