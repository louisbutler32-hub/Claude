# 7 Ways the Earth Kills You Without Warning

## Title

7 Ways the Earth Kills You Without Warning

Alternates:

- How Much Warning Would You Actually Get?
- Nobody Had Time to Run

## Description

Seven things the planet does that give you almost no notice, ranked by the only number that matters when one happens: how much warning you get.

A lake in Cameroon that exhaled in 1986 and killed 1,746 people in a night. A cloud that destroyed a city in under a minute, and the one man who survived it because he was locked in a cell. A bedroom floor in Florida that opened at half past eleven. Lakes in Scotland that started swinging because of an earthquake two thousand kilometres away. A solar storm that set telegraph paper on fire in 1859, and what the same storm would do to a grid that now exists.

And two that are not what you have been told. The supervolcano everybody is frightened of would give the most warning of anything on the list. And the one that gives you none at all comes from outside the solar system entirely.

No footage, no stock, no music. Every frame is drawn in code.
