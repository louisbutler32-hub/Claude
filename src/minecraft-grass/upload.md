## Which version to upload

| file | music | copyright |
|---|---|---|
| `finally-reach-grass.mp4` | an original choir swell and acoustic melody made for this Short | none — nothing licensed in it |
| `finally-reach-grass-no-music.mp4` | none — add a song in YouTube's Shorts sound picker | licensed through YouTube |

Songs that fit the breakthrough, through the sound picker on the no-music cut:
**"Here Comes the Sun" (The Beatles)** — the obvious one; start it so "Here comes
the sun" lands as the last block breaks, at **0:07.4**, and cut it at **0:11.7**
when the creeper shows up. Runner-up: **"Walking on
Sunshine" (Katrina and the Waves)**. Or a choir sting like **"Hallelujah Chorus"**
if you want the joke louder.

To bake a song into a private cut instead, put it in `public/audio/src/` and
run `python3 scripts/build-grass-audio.py --song FILE.mp3 --at SECONDS` (`--at`
is where in the track the breakthrough sits, so the song's big moment lands as
the last block breaks). That cut would be claimed.

## How it differs from his

His video is a front view of a stick figure in a dark shaft, looking up. Ours
is a cutaway of the ground:

- You watch Oofy pillar up through stone, then dirt, a block at a time, the
  camera riding up with him and the rhythm speeding up as he gets into it, with
  a live Y readout (Y: −52 → Y: 64).
- You can see the sunlit surface, a cow and the sky above him before he can —
  that is the joke.
- The last block leaks light at its edges, he sees it, holds his breath, and
  breaks through into a meadow.
- The payoff: he hauls himself out, squints, takes it in, and lets himself
  fall flat on his back in the grass, with birds, a cow and a butterfly.
- The twist: the camera cuts to first person, flat on his back. A creeper
  walks up from the horizon, looms over the lens, hisses, and blows up into
  the "You died!" screen. The music stops dead when it appears.
