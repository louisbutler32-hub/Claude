## Description

```
Can you guess the fruit from its shadow? 🍎🍌🍓

Peekaboo! Pebblo is hiding in the bushes — and so is a mystery fruit. A
shadow rises up… what could it be? Guess along with Pebblo, find out where
each fruit grows, and watch out for Munch, the hungry little monster who
gobbles every one! Every fruit you name fills one more space on the board —
can you collect all 12 with Pebblo, Pip, Bloom, Tock and Wisp?

🍉 WHAT YOUR LITTLE ONE WILL LEARN
• 12 fruit names — apple, banana, orange, strawberry, grapes, watermelon,
  pear, pineapple, cherries, lemon, peach and kiwi
• Where fruit grows — on trees, on vines, up a palm, low on the ground
• Shape recognition, by matching each shadow to the fruit it hides
• Counting from 1 to 12 as the board fills up

👶 Made for toddlers, preschoolers and kindergarten. Nice and slow, with a long
pause after every shadow so there's time to shout out the answer.
```
