## Upload settings

Category **Gaming** (game: Minecraft), language English, **Short** (it is
9:16 and under 60 seconds, so YouTube files it as one automatically).

Audience: this is a cartoon about a video game with nothing in it for adults
only, and it sits on a kids' channel, so mark it **Yes, made for kids**. That
is the channel owner's call — a general-audience gaming channel would say no.

Thumbnail: `thumbnail-minecraft-short.jpg` (`npm run minecraft:thumb`),
1080×1920. Shorts mostly show a frame from the video itself; the thumbnail is
what the channel page and the share card use.

Sound: the render's audio track is whatever sits in
`public/audio/minecraft-mix.mp3` (see the README). If the sound is a YouTube
library track, add it in the Shorts editor at upload instead of baking it in.
