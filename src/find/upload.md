# Upload copy — Find 10 (hidden-object Shorts)

Two Shorts, one format: a scene, ten of the same animal strung along its
path toward the horizon, "Find 10 …" up top. Both ship on **Pebblo Pebble**,
both are 9:16 and 10 s, both get flagged **made for kids** on upload.

No chapter list — a 10-second Short can't carry one (YouTube needs at least
three chapters of 10 s each).
