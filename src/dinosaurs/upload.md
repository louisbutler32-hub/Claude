## Description

```
Can you guess the dinosaur from its shadow? 🦖🦕🌋

A mystery shadow rises out of the bushes… what could it be? Guess along with
us, find out where each dinosaur lived, and hear the sound it makes! Every
dinosaur you name fills one more space on the board — can you collect all 12?

🌿 WHAT YOUR LITTLE ONE WILL LEARN
• 12 dinosaur names — T-Rex, Triceratops, Pterodactyl, Stegosaurus,
  Velociraptor, Brachiosaurus, Ankylosaurus, Spinosaurus, Parasaurolophus,
  Diplodocus, Iguanodon and Allosaurus
• The sound each one makes — ROAR, snort, honk, screech and more
• Where dinosaurs lived — the forest, the swamp, by the volcano
• Shape recognition, by matching each shadow to the dinosaur it hides
• Counting from 1 to 12 as the board fills up

👶 Made for toddlers, preschoolers and kindergarten. Nice and slow, with a long
pause after every shadow so there's time to shout out the answer.
```
