## Description

```
Can you guess the vehicle from its shadow? 🚗🚒🚂

A mystery shadow rises out of the bushes… what could it be? Guess along with
us, find out where each vehicle goes, and listen to the sound it makes! Every
vehicle you name fills one more space on the board — can you collect all 12?

🚦 WHAT YOUR LITTLE ONE WILL LEARN
• 12 vehicle names — car, airplane, bus, fire engine, boat, police car,
  train, tractor, helicopter, digger, bicycle and motorcycle
• The sound each one makes — vroom, choo choo, nee-naw, whoosh and more
• Where vehicles go — the road, the sky, the tracks, the water
• Shape recognition, by matching each shadow to the vehicle it hides
• Counting from 1 to 12 as the board fills up

👶 Made for toddlers, preschoolers and kindergarten. Nice and slow, with a long
pause after every shadow so there's time to shout out the answer.
```
