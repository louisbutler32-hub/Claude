## Description

```
Can you guess the fruit from its shadow? 🍎🍌🍇

This episode uses real fruit photos instead of drawings. A mystery shadow
rises out of the bushes… what could it be? Guess along with us, find out
where each fruit grows, and watch out — here comes the crocodile! Every
fruit you name fills one more space on the board — can you collect all 12?

🍉 WHAT YOUR LITTLE ONE WILL LEARN
• 12 fruit names — apple, banana, orange, strawberry, grapes, watermelon,
  pineapple, kiwi, mango, cherries, peach and pear
• Where each fruit grows — on a tree, on a vine, or low on the ground
• Shape recognition, by matching each shadow to the fruit it hides
• Counting from 1 to 12 as the board fills up

👶 Made for toddlers, preschoolers and kindergarten. Nice and slow, with a long
pause after every shadow so there's time to shout out the answer.
```
