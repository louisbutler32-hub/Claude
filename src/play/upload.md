The race has four endings. Render the others with
`--props='{"winner":"capy"}'` (or `"bun"`, `"mint"`) and post them as a
series. The title can stay the same, because nobody can tell who wins from
the first frame.

## Upload settings (both)

| field | value |
|---|---|
| Category | Entertainment |
| Audience | **Yes, it's made for kids.** Pebblo Pebble's audience is under 13, so this is required. |
| Shorts | Vertical 1080×1920, 28 seconds |
| Playlist | ALL OUR LEARNING VIDEOS |

Made-for-kids turns comments off. The race Short's "tell us who you picked"
line only earns replies if the Short goes out on a general-audience channel
instead.
