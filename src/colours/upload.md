## Description

Ooh, what colour is it? 🔴🟠🟡🟢🔵🟣

A colourful friend pops up on screen — can you name its colour? Every colour
brings along two more friends from around Pebblo Pebble who share that very
same colour, so you'll spot red on a fire engine, blue on a whale, and green
on a dinosaur! Every colour you learn fills one more space on the board — can
you collect all 12, right through to the rainbow at the end?

🎨 WHAT YOUR LITTLE ONE WILL LEARN
• 12 colours — red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, pink, brown, black,
  white, grey and rainbow
• Colour recognition, matching one colour across totally different objects
  and animals
• New vocabulary — tomato, carrot, corn, whale, eggplant, elephant and more
• Counting from 1 to 12 as the board fills up

👶 Made for toddlers, preschoolers and kindergarten. Nice and slow, with a
clear colour name and two colourful friends every time.
