# Upload copy — POV: Your first night in Minecraft

First person the whole way (the character is never shown), on the stealth
pluck of Sneaky Snitch. Every pluck lands on the picture: the block going
in, dusk, the zombies, the window, the three pounding thumps, the light, the
block coming out.

## Before you upload

- The music is Sneaky Snitch (Kevin MacLeod). Check the licence you hold
  for it; if you don't want a claim, use `pov-night-nomusic.mp4` and add a
  song from the Shorts sound picker. The effects are your Minecraft sound files
  plus original synthesised groans.
