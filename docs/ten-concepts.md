## 01 · 7 Ways to Become a Mummy
*Alt: You Have 72 Hours to Become a Mummy*

Cold open: you have about seventy-two hours before you start to smell. Here
is how to last four thousand years instead.

1. Ice. Ötzi, freeze-dried at altitude for 5,300 years.
2. Bog. Tollund Man. Sphagnan tans your skin while acid and no oxygen stop the bacteria.
3. Desert. Pure desiccation. The Chinchorro beat Egypt by two thousand years.
4. Salt. The Iranian salt men, pickled in a collapsed mine.
5. Natron. The Egyptian method. Seventy days, organs out, brain through the nose.
6. Self-mummification. Sokushinbutsu. Three years of nuts and bark, then lacquer tea, then sealed in while still alive.
7. Plastination. Acetone swaps for polymer.

## 02 · What's Living on Your Face Right Now
*Alt: You Are Covered in Animals*

Cold open: there are eight-legged animals living in your eyelashes.

1. Demodex folliculorum, head-down in the hair follicles.
2. Demodex brevis, deeper, in the oil glands.
3. Waste accumulates until they die and rupture. *(built video corrects this — the 2022 genome study found the anus they were long said to lack)*
4. Inherited from whoever held you as a baby.
5. The gut: roughly as many bacterial cells as human ones.
6. The navel: 2,368 species across volunteers.
7. Almost none of it is harmful, and you cannot get rid of it.

## 03 · How Long Would You Last on Every Planet?
*Alt: Ranking the Solar System by How Fast It Kills You*

1. Venus. Ninety times our pressure at 460°C.
2. Mercury. Vacuum, and a 600-degree swing between sides.
3. Mars. Around two minutes. Below the Armstrong limit your saliva boils.
4. The gas giants. No surface. You fall until the pressure ends it.
5. Io. Radiation kills you before the volcanoes do.
6. Titan. The twist — pressure is survivable.
7. The Sun.

## 04 · Your Body, Hour by Hour, After You Die
*Alt: The Eight Weeks After Your Heart Stops*

Cold open: two minutes ago your heart stopped. Your bacteria have not noticed yet.

1. Minute 1. Livor mortis.
2. Hour 2–6. Rigor mortis — muscles run out of the ATP needed to release.
3. Hour 12. Peak stiffness, then reversal.
4. Day 2. Autolysis.
5. Day 4. Bloat.
6. Week 2. Active decay.
7. The fork: wet and airless, you turn to grave wax instead.

## 05 · What Ancient People Thought Fossils Were
*Alt: Everyone Who Found a Fossil Was Wrong for 2,000 Years*

1. Griffins. Protoceratops skulls eroding out of the Gobi.
2. Cyclopes. Dwarf elephant skulls.
3. Dragon bones, ground up for medicine.
4. Thunderstones. Belemnites.
5. St Hilda's snakes. Whitby ammonites, sold with heads carved on.
6. Tongue stones. Shark teeth.
7. 1667. Steno dissects a shark and the whole thing collapses.

## 06 · 6 Animals That Survive Being Eaten
*Alt: Getting Swallowed Is Not Always the End*

1. Bombardier beetle. Sprayed back out of the toad alive.
2. Water beetle. *Regimbartia attenuata* walks the length of a frog.
3. Eel. Punches out through the stomach wall.
4. Snails. Ride through birds intact.
5. Seeds that will not germinate unless digested first.
6. Tardigrade, on a technicality.

## 07 · 7 Ways the Earth Kills You Without Warning
*Alt: Nobody Had Time to Run*

Cold open: none of these gave anyone time to run.

1. Limnic eruption. Lake Nyos, 1986.
2. Pyroclastic flow. Faster than a car, hot enough to boil you inside your own skin.
3. Sinkholes. Florida limestone dissolving under houses that are still standing.
4. Solar storm. A Carrington-class event on a grid that did not exist in 1859.
5. Seiche. A lake sloshing like a bathtub after a distant earthquake.
6. Supervolcano. Toba, and the genetic bottleneck argument.
7. Gamma-ray burst. The one you would never see coming.

## 08 · What Would Actually Happen If You Fell In
*Alt: You Would Not Sink Into Lava*

1. Lava. Three times denser than water — you land on top of it.
2. Quicksand. You float. The danger is the tide.
3. A grain silo. Entrapment in about five seconds.
4. A tar pit. How La Brea collected a hundred thousand skeletons.
5. The Mariana Trench. A thousand atmospheres.
6. A black hole.

## 09 · 8 Things That Are Basically Immortal
*Alt: Nothing Told These Species They Were Supposed to Die*

1. *Turritopsis dohrnii*, the jellyfish that reverts to its juvenile form.
2. Hydra. No measurable increase in death rate with age.
3. Naked mole rat. The same, in a mammal.
4. Greenland shark. Roughly four hundred years.
5. Ming the clam. 507 years, killed by the scientists counting its rings.
6. Pando. One aspen organism, 47,000 stems.
7. Bristlecone pines.
8. Endoliths, which may divide once a century.

## 10 · The 5 Deadliest Things in Your House
*Alt: You Are Most Likely to Die in This Room*

1. Radon.
2. Carbon monoxide. Binds ~200× more readily than oxygen.
3. Bleach and ammonia — chloramine gas.
4. Stairs.
5. The dryer. Lint, and why the vent matters more than the filter.
