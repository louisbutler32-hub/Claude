## The two engines

**No bridge / the gap.** "Have you ever wondered why there is no X between
A and B?" Distance first (a measure on the map), then a comparison to
something that *was* built, then the real reasons as a diagram or an icon
row, then the money. Ends on a fact, not a call to action.

**The deal.** "Not many people know that…" A territory wears one flag, the
price lands as a callout, the inflation-adjusted price lands next, and the
payoff is what the land turned out to be worth — the biggest number last.

## Numbers to verify before scripting

- **Sicily**: Strait of Messina ~3.3 km; the bridge approved in 2023 at
  ~€13.5 billion would be the longest suspension span in the world; the
  1908 Messina earthquake; the currents of Scylla and Charybdis.
- **Greenland**: the 1946 offer of $100 million in gold; 1867 interest;
  the island is ~2.17 million km², 56,000 people; 80% ice sheet.
- **Bir Tawil**: 2,060 km², claimed by neither Egypt nor Sudan because of
  two incompatible 1899/1902 boundaries — each country's preferred line
  gives it the Hala'ib Triangle instead.
- **Point Nemo**: 48°52.6′S 123°23.6′W, ~2,688 km from the nearest land;
  the ISS passes 400 km overhead, closer than any human on land.
- **Hans Island**: 1.3 km², the 2022 agreement, the whisky and schnapps.
- **Bolivia**: lost 400 km of coast in the War of the Pacific (1879–84);
  keeps a navy on Lake Titicaca.
- **Overseas Highway**: 182 km, 42 bridges; the Seven Mile Bridge.
