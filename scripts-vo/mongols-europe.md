# Why The Mongols Never Conquered Europe

**Format** vertical short, 1080x1920 · **Runtime** ~78s · **Structure** cold
open at the mystery, rewind, escalate, callback, payoff, twist.

**First frame**: Mongol red filling the map, arrow tip sitting just short of
Vienna. Text: `50 KM FROM VIENNA`

---

## Hooks — pick one, they're interchangeable

**A — the cold open (used below).** Opens *at* the unexplained moment. The
question is planted before any history has to be taught.
> It's 1242. The Mongol army is fifty kilometres from Vienna. Nothing in
> Europe has stopped it. Then it turns around and goes home.

**B — the atrocity fact.** Hardest opening image, best for a cold audience.
> Europe lost two entire armies in forty-eight hours. Four hundred miles
> apart. And that was the good news.

**C — the contradiction.** Attacks the assumption directly.
> Europe didn't stop the Mongols. Europe lost. Every single battle. So why
> isn't Europe Mongol?

No hedging in the first line. "Not once, in the open field" is accurate and
it is a retention killer — qualify in the body, never in the hook.

## Why this should hold

- The mystery is planted at **13 seconds**, before any history has to be
  taught. The audience is now waiting for an answer instead of deciding
  whether to care.
- Everything between 0:17 and 1:02 exists to make the withdrawal feel
  impossible. Escalation on a visible clock: Dec 1240 → 9 April → 11 April
  → winter → spring.
- **"Two kingdoms. Forty-eight hours."** lands at 0:49, right on the
  scroll-off cliff, and it is the best fact in the video.
- **"Which is where we came in"** at 1:02 closes the loop opened at 0:00.
  That callback is the moment the video feels designed rather than listed.
- Two payoffs. The funeral answers it; "Or it wasn't" re-opens it and buys
  the last 20 seconds. Ending on an unsettled argument is what drives
  comments.
- Last line restates the hook's thesis, so a rewatch loops cleanly.

## Facts, and how far they can be pushed

- **Wiener Neustadt**, ~50 km from Vienna — raiding parties, not a siege.
  "Fifty kilometres from Vienna" is fair; "at the gates of Vienna" is not.
- **Kyiv** fell 6 December 1240. The two hundred houses is Giovanni da Pian
  del Carpine riding through in 1246 — a real eyewitness, not legend.
- **Legnica 9 April, Mohi 11 April 1241**, roughly 400 miles apart. Solid,
  and the spine of the video.
- **Every field army lost** — Kalka 1223, Chmielnik, Legnica, Mohi. Several
  fortified places did hold (Esztergom's citadel, Székesfehérvár, Trogir),
  so keep the claim to armies in the field, in the body copy.
- **Ogedei died 11 December 1241**; Batu withdrew in spring 1242. The
  succession explanation is traditional and contested.
- **The weather** is Büntgen & Di Cosmo, *Scientific Reports*, 2016. Present
  it as the rival theory it is — "the tree rings say" — never as settled.
- Hungary's losses: 15–25% of the population, higher on the plain. Quote a
  range or don't quote a number.
